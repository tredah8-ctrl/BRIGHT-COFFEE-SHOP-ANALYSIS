# BRIGHT-COFFEE-SHOP-ANALYSIS

https://brightcoffee-viz.lovable.app


📌 Project Overview
This project analyses the **Bright Coffee Shop Sales Dataset** to help the business understand its sales performance and identify opportunities to increase revenue. The analysis was completed using SQL for data preparation and Power BI for data visualization.
The goal is to provide business insights that support better decision-making through interactive dashboards and data-driven recommendations.

Business Objectives
 Which products generate the most revenue?
- What time of day does the coffee shop perform best?
- What are the sales trends across products and time?
- What recommendations can improve sales performance?


📂 Dataset

The dataset contains coffee shop transaction records, including:

- Transaction ID
- Transaction Date
- Transaction Time
- Product Name
- Product Category
- Quantity Sold
- Unit Price
- Total Sales

🧹 Data Preparation

The following data cleaning and preparation steps were completed:

- Removed duplicate records
- Handled missing values
- Standardised product names and categories
- Converted data types
- Formatted dates and times
- Created Month, Day and Hour columns
- Created time-of-day groups (Morning, Afternoon and Evening).

📊 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- Total Revenue KPI
- Total Transactions KPI
- Revenue by Product
- Revenue by Product Category
- Sales by Time of Day
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top Selling Products
- Interactive Filters and Slicer

  💡 Key Insights

- A small number of products generate most of the revenue.
- Coffee products are the biggest contributors to total sales.
- Sales are highest during peak business hours.
- Revenue changes across different months, showing seasonal trends.
- Some products perform significantly better than others.


 ✅ Recommendations

- Keep high-performing products well stocked.
- Promote slow-selling products through discounts and bundle offers.
- Schedule more staff during busy periods.
- Run promotions during quieter hours.
- Monitor dashboard performance regularly to support business decisions.

 📈 Project Workflow

1. Understand the business problem
2. Explore the dataset
3. Clean and prepare the data
4. Analyse sales using SQL
5. Create interactive Power BI dashboards
6. Generate business insights
7. Present recommendations
